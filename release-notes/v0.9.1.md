## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.9.1: spoons, now optional

**Turn off the energy budget.** Autism & ADHD's spoons can now be switched off: You › What you
track, then turn off "Energy budget (spoons)" under Autism & ADHD. With it off, spoons are gone
from the Autism & ADHD page, from Care, from + and from Today's "Right now" and timeline. The
social battery stays if you use it. Turn it back on there, or from "More you can turn on" on the
Autism & ADHD page, any time; nothing you logged is lost.
