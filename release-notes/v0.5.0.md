## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.5.0: what you're wearing, and what's in your food

**Bladder & bowel: what you're wearing, and changes in one step.** The diary now opens on
**Wearing now**: the product you have on, since when, and how long you usually wear that one,
with a big **Change** button.

A change is one sheet:
- **Taking off:** already set to what you're wearing.
- **How it was:** how full it was (dry to leaked through), whether it leaked through, clothes or
  bedding, skin, and the weight if you weigh them.
- **Putting on:** your usual product first, or "Nothing for now".
- **When:** now, or earlier.

The diary then reads like "Night briefs → Day pants · Full · worn 7 h".

- **How your products go** compares each product: how long you usually wear it, how full it
  tends to get, and how often it leaks through, by day and night.
- **Supplies count down by what you put on.** Your earlier entries count just as before.
- **An optional change reminder** after the hours you choose, on the phone too. It only ever
  says "Time for a check", and it can wait until morning.
- **On Today:** the Bladder & bowel card shows what you're wearing, the Bladder quick button
  opens the Change sheet, and Up next says when a change is due.

**Vitamins and minerals.** 24 micronutrients come along with the foods that carry them: Open Food
Facts and barcodes (many products list them), and your own foods' new "More nutrients".
- **Food page:** a **Vitamins & minerals** card shows your day against adult reference intakes,
  using the same female, male or average values you chose for your calorie formula, with more
  folate, iron and iodine in pregnancy. Sodium, cholesterol and trans fat count against a limit.
- **Honest about gaps:** it says how many of the day's foods carried any, and never counts a
  missing value as none.
- **Your own targets:** set any target in Settings, and chart any nutrient in Trends.
- **One gentle weekly insight** when something's been low, but only once enough of your food
  carries that nutrient.
- **Health Connect:** your food goes there with its micronutrients.
- **Food photos** now estimate sugar, saturated fat and sodium too (never vitamins: they can't
  be judged from a photo).
- **Eating-disorder recovery:** none of these numbers show.

Also in this version:
- CSV exports no longer turn 700 kcal into "7".
- A newer server can add nutrients without older apps tripping over them.
