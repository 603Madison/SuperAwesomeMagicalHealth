## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.7.0: the last 24 hours, as your ring recorded it

**Last 24 hours.** A new screen with everything your devices recorded over the last day, on one
timeline. Your night is shaded across every chart, so you can see how it lines up.
- **Sleep:** each night or nap with its stages across its own hours (deep, REM, light and awake),
  how long you were asleep, and the time in each stage.
- **Heart rate** through the day and night, with the lowest, highest, average and latest.
- **Blood oxygen, heart rate variability and stress**, each with its lowest, highest, average and
  latest, as your ring measured them.
- **Breathing rate and skin temperature** when a device sends them (in °F if you use imperial
  units).
- **Steps** hour by hour, with the total. With a ring and a phone, the same walk is never counted
  twice.
- **One device or all of them.** "All devices", or just your ring. Open it from a device in
  Devices and it starts with that one.

On the phone, open it from Today's Sleep card, the Sleep chart in Trends, or a device in Devices.
Nothing recorded yet? It says when the device last synced, with a Sync now button. On the web,
it's on the Sleep page, under each device in Devices, and from Today's Heart card.

Like the other screens, its sections can be rearranged or hidden. It's only ever your own: it
isn't shown to anyone you share with.
