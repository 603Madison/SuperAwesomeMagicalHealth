## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.9.3: Compare devices you can trust

Compare devices was making confident claims from bad comparisons. Now it compares only like with
like, and says when it's too early to tell.

**Steps are compared only over the hours both devices were counting.**
- A phone counts only while it's carried; a ring counts all day. Setting a whole day against a
  whole day mostly measured the hours the phone sat on a table.
- An hour counts when both devices counted at least 20 steps in it. A step or three from picking
  the phone up doesn't count.
- It says how many hours that was, and gives the difference as a share: "In the 5 hours both were
  counting, the phone counted 45% fewer steps than the ring".

**Too early? It says so.**
- With fewer than 3 nights (or days) together, Compare devices says "only 1 day so far: a few more
  will show what's usual" instead of stating a difference.
- Body's Compare devices row says "Only 1 night together so far" until then.

**The phone has a name.**
- Health Connect's own step count used to show up as a long code like "Jb58517a34cd…".
- It's now called "Phone", with your phone's model. The one already saved is renamed on its next
  sync, on the server, even before you update the app.
- A name you gave a device yourself is never changed.

**The ring's steps cover the right hour.**
- The R02 keeps each hour's steps together. The app used to file them under that hour's first 15
  minutes; from the next sync they cover the whole hour.
- The 24-hour steps chart is more accurate, and a walk counted by both the phone and the ring is
  less likely to be counted twice in the day's total.
