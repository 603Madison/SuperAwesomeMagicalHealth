## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.6.1: food photos and progress photos work at full size

**Snap your food works again.** A photo straight from a phone was turned away with "Something went
wrong on the server", because the server only accepted uploads far smaller than a real photo. Food
photos are now estimated as they should be.

**Progress photos** had the same limit, and now take full-size photos too (up to 20 MB, as before).

This is a server fix: update the server, and the phone app needs nothing new.
