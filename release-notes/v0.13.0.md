## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.13.0: guided meditation for bedtime

**Update the app and the server together.**

- **Five guided meditations** for winding down at night: Wind down, Body scan, Slow breathing,
  Drifting off and Gratitude, each in 5, 10 or 15 minutes. Each begins by saying eyes open is
  fine, you can shift position, and you can stop anytime.
- **Read aloud by your own device's voice, offline.** No audio is downloaded and no words go to a
  cloud voice. Without a built-in voice, the words are shown instead, or choose "Text only".
- **Made for bedtime:** a dark, dim screen, a breathing circle to follow, soft chimes, and on the
  phone it keeps going with the screen off. "Then fade out" ends in darkness, with no chime.
- **A nightly reminder** if you'd like one ("Time to wind down", at a time you choose), and a
  Wind down item in Right now around then.
- **Your sessions** go on your timeline, with an optional "How do you feel?" afterwards. Once
  there are enough nights, it says how you've slept after meditating, as an observation only.
- **Where:** Care › Meditation, or + › Meditate.
- **Private:** never offered to the assistant; shares see sessions only if Mind is shared.
