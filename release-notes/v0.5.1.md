## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.5.1: accidents go with what you're wearing

**Bladder & bowel reads product by product.** An accident now belongs to whatever you had on at
the time, and the diary shows it that way.

- **Wearing now** shows how full what you have on is (a small pad that fills, "Wet", or "Dry so
  far"), with the accidents since it went on listed right there. **Log an accident** and **Change**
  sit side by side.
- **Logging an accident** while something's on says "In your Megamax Airlock" at the top, with
  "Not this" if it wasn't. "How full it is now" starts where it already is, so you only nudge it up.
- **Save and change** saves the accident and goes straight to the Change sheet. That sheet takes
  off what you're wearing, already set to how the accident left it.
- **The day's list** shows each product as one item: how full it was when it came off, how long
  it was worn and what went on next, with its accidents underneath. An overnight brief keeps the
  accident from before midnight with it. What you have on now stays in its card at the top, not
  repeated below.

Also in this version:
- The quick row leaves out Change while Wearing now has its own.
- Putting something on with nothing to take off no longer counts as a change.
- The "Changed it afterwards" switch is gone when the diary keeps track of products. "Save and
  change" does that job now, without counting a product twice.
- Today's Bladder & bowel card says how it is: "Wet · 2 accidents".
- The AI connector's day says how full what's on is, and how many accidents since it went on.
- On an older server, the phone and web show the day as a plain list, as before.
