## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.8.0: a chest strap, health alerts, and a tidier app

**Health alerts.** The app now watches what your devices send and tells you when something looks
out of the ordinary for you, is worth a doctor's look, or could need care now.
- **A heart rate that stays high at rest**: above 120 for 10 minutes by default, not while you're
  walking, working out or getting your breath back. It's urgent when a chest strap, or two devices
  together, see it above 150.
- **A heart rate that stays low while you're awake** (40 by default), and a very low one asleep.
- **An irregular rhythm**, from a chest strap's beat-to-beat timing. A ring or watch can't tell
  this, so it needs a strap that's recording.
- **Blood oxygen** that stays low while you're awake, or dips night after night.
- **The signs of coming down with something**: your resting heart rate up, HRV down, and
  temperature and breathing up together, against your own usual. It allows for the second half
  of a cycle.
- **A resting heart rate that has stayed up** for three days.

Each alert says how soon to act, the readings behind it, and what else it could be. Anything that
could need care now says when to call emergency services.
- **Where they show:** they come to the phone as notifications, with no push service involved,
  and to your browser if you've turned that on. They sit at the top of Today, behind a bell, and
  in Health alerts, where you can put one away and say why.
- **Settings:** you set the heart rate and blood oxygen lines yourself.
- **Elsewhere:** they turn into questions for your next appointment, and your AI connector can
  read them. They're never shared.

They're a heads-up from consumer devices, not a diagnosis, and they can't catch everything. If you
feel unwell, trust that over any reading.

**Heart rate chest straps.** Any standard Bluetooth heart rate strap works, read by the phone app
itself, with no vendor app or account. That includes the Polar H9 and H10, Garmin HRM, Wahoo
TICKR, Coospo and Decathlon straps.
- **Recording:** add it in Devices, press Record before bed, and it records all night alongside
  your ring, reconnecting by itself if it drops.
- **What it records:** heart rate every minute, HRV every five minutes from the true time between
  beats, and the rhythm check.
- **How it counts:** a strap reads your heart's own electrical beat, so wherever it measured, its
  heart rate is the one used, and it counts most for resting heart rate and HRV.

**Compare devices.** Two devices' nights side by side, a ring against a strap say, compared over
only the minutes both were measuring.
- **What's compared:** time asleep and its stages, when you fell asleep and woke, heart rate
  overnight and its lowest, HRV, blood oxygen, breathing, temperature and steps.
- **The result:** how far apart they read on average and how steady that gap is, with each night's
  hypnograms one above the other.

It's a good way to learn how far to trust a ring. Find it in Devices, or More › Compare devices.

**Where your data goes.** A page listing every way anything about you can leave your server: the
food database, the weather, Mealie, the assistant, AI connector links, webhooks, browser
notifications, the weekly email, shares, Home Assistant and signing in.
- **For each one:** who's at the other end, what goes and when, and a switch to pause it for
  yourself. Pausing keeps its setup, and nothing goes until you turn it back on.
- **On the phone:** it also says what stays on the phone. The ring and strap talk only to it, and
  Health Connect is only written to if you chose that.

Everything else stays on your server, and the apps load nothing from anywhere else.

**A tidier app.** The phone app and the web app at phone width now share the same five tabs:
Today, 24 hours, Health, Trends and More.
- **24 hours:** Last 24 hours is one tap away.
- **Health:** every feature you use, Food included.
- **More:** gathers your records (Health alerts, Your week, Reports & export), your devices, the
  assistant and Settings. Settings is now short pages instead of one long scroll.
- **Smaller fixes:** every page that isn't a tab has a back arrow, cards that open a tab switch to
  it, and each place has one name everywhere. Your arrangements carry over.

**Also**
- Open Food Facts barcode lookups through the AI connector now respect the server's food
  database switch.
- Mealie and the assistant say plainly when they're paused, instead of failing.
