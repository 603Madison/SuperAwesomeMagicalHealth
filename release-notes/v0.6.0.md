## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.6.0: your Mealie recipes in Food, and no more stray popups

**Mealie, in Food.** If you keep your recipes in [Mealie](https://mealie.io), connect it in
**Settings › Connections › Mealie** with its address and an API token (in Mealie: your profile,
then API tokens).
- **Searching for food** now shows **Your Mealie** next to your own foods and Open Food Facts.
  Each recipe comes with one serving's nutrition, taken from the recipe in Mealie, and says how many
  servings it makes. Choose it like any other food.
- **The Food page** has a **Meal plan** card with what's planned in Mealie for the day, in meal
  order. One tap adds a planned meal to its meal, and it shows as **Logged** after.
- **Recipes with no nutrition in Mealie** are shown, with a way to open them in Mealie so you can add
  it there.
- **A recipe you've logged** joins your foods. Next time it's logged it uses the recipe's numbers
  as they are in Mealie then.
- **Nothing is ever changed in Mealie.** Your token is kept sealed on your server and is never
  shown again. Someone you share with never reaches your Mealie.
- **In eating disorder recovery**, no calories or macros show here either.

**No more "StandaloneCoroutine was cancelled" popups.** When a screen refreshed (say, right after
you logged something), the phone app stopped the load already in progress and started a fresh
one. It then wrongly showed the stopped load as an error. Stopped work is now just stopped,
everywhere in the app, and that message can't reach a popup any more.
