## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.7.1: the last 24 hours, with every library up to date

This version brings two lines of work together:
- **The Last 24 hours screen from 0.7.0.** Everything your devices recorded over the last day, on
  one timeline: each night's sleep stages, heart rate, blood oxygen, HRV, stress, breathing, skin
  temperature and steps, from your ring alone or all your devices. Open it from Today's Sleep card,
  the Sleep chart in Trends, or a device in Devices; on the web, from the Sleep page and Devices.
- **The library updates from 0.6.3 and 0.6.4.** Security fixes to the server's, web app's and phone
  app's libraries, and every dependency brought up to date.

0.7.0 was built without those updates and with a version number the phone saw as older than 0.6.4,
so it wouldn't install over it. 0.7.1 has everything. Update the server and the phone app.
