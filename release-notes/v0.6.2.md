## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.6.2: safer, steadier, and reminders you can count on

A careful review of the server, the web app and the phone app, and fixes for what it found.

**Your reminders**
- **The phone app kept clearing reminders.** If it couldn't reach your server when it started
  (out of the house, on a plane, the server restarting), it cleared medication, habit, appointment
  and bladder reminders. Routine reminders were cleared on every cold start. Now they stay put.
- **On time, and none missed.** Reminders arrive on time even while the phone is asleep. When one
  comes late, anything that came due in the meantime still shows.
- **Every few days means every few days.** A medication taken every few days (a patch, an injection)
  without a start date reminded daily. It now counts from the day it was added, and adherence no
  longer counts days before a medication existed.
- **Glucose alerts.** Low and high glucose alerts are now actually sent.
- **Daylight saving.** Reminders keep to the clock on the days it changes.

**Your privacy**
- **Names in reminders.** "Show names in reminders" now means exactly that. Off (the default),
  reminders say only "Time for your 8:00 medications", even on a lock screen set to show everything.
  The same goes for habits, routines, appointments and the focus timer.
- **Sharing shows only what you share.** Someone you share Appointments with no longer gets
  suggested questions built from your other features. Supplies, lab results, progress photos, your
  goals and your calorie formula also follow what you've shared.
- **The AI connector's log** records which tools were used, never what you wrote in them. The
  assistant is no longer told which private features you keep from it.
- **Browser and device leftovers.** Signing out in a browser stops its reminders, and nothing of
  yours (settings, Mealie, the assistant's chats) carries over to the next person who signs in
  there. Assistant chats now last only as long as the browser tab.
- **Plain http.** The phone app warns before signing in to a server over plain http, unless the
  address is on your home network.

**Your data**
- **Unsent data.** Data the phone app hasn't sent yet is no longer thrown away by a Wi-Fi sign-in
  page or a proxy's error. It waits, and only ever goes to the account it belongs to.
- **Food numbers stay hidden.** With food numbers hidden (eating disorder recovery), a slow or
  failed connection no longer makes calories appear on the web.
- **Today follows your time zone** on the web, and moves to the new day if the page was left open
  overnight, so water and coffee never land on yesterday.
- **Steady counts.** Today's numbers no longer stick to old values when something's logged while
  they load. Taps at the same moment (watch and phone) no longer lose a pad or a dose from stock.
- **Past days and reports.** Opening a past day from before a habit started no longer fails, and a
  report for an earlier period uses that period's bladder and bowel weeks.

**Under the hood**
- The server turns away requests built to run it out of memory, and checks where it connects for
  webhooks, Mealie and browser notifications: never itself or a cloud's internal services, home
  networks as the admin allows.
- Signing in can't be steered off-site.
- Wrong passwords typed elsewhere can't lock your account.
- A saved Mealie token, mail password or sign-on secret isn't sent to a new address until you
  enter it again.
- A newer server no longer trips up an older phone app over a feature it doesn't know yet.
- Mealie reads "1046 kJ / 250 kcal" as 250.

Update the server and the phone app together.
