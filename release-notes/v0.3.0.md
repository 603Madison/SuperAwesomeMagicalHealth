## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.3.0: arranged your way

**Arrange any screen.** Every screen, on the phone and the web, has an **Arrange** button: move
its sections up and down, hide the ones you don't use, and "Put back as it was" whenever you
like. It's saved on your server, so Today, its quick actions and your list of features look the
same on your phone and in the browser. Trends' charts, Mind, Bladder & bowel, Medications and the
rest all arrange the same way.

**Turn water off.** Settings › Features, under Food: with water off, it's gone from Today, Food,
your goals, drinks, habits, Trends, insights, the weekly digest, the report, the watch, Home
Assistant, the AI connector and Health Connect. Drinks still count caffeine and alcohol. What
you've logged stays saved (and in the CSV export), and comes back if you turn water on again.

**A cleaner, calmer interface:**

- Today shows **a few quick actions** for the features you use, with the rest under **More**, and
  you choose which.
- Cards with nothing in them yet are a single line; the explanations live on each feature's own
  page instead.
- No scores yet? One line instead of three empty rings.
- Every card's header works the same way: its title, at most one action, and an arrow to open it.
- Low supplies show once, as their own card.
- The web's sidebar is grouped (Today & Trends, Your health, Records), and Settings on both apps is
  grouped into clear sections (You, Features, Reminders & sharing, Connections, Appearance…).
- On phones, quick actions wrap instead of scrolling out of sight.
