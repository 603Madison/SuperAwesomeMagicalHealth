## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.2.0: yours, in detail

**Pick what you're tracking, and the apps fit around it.** Choose the conditions and goals you
live with or want to work on (overactive bladder, IBS, endometriosis, PCOS, ADHD, autism,
depression, high blood pressure, diabetes, transition, pregnancy, losing weight, sleeping
better… or your own), and the features and fields that fit turn on by themselves. Everything else
stays out of the way. Each field can still be shown or hidden by hand, with a note of what brought
it.

**Bladder & bowel, as detailed as you like:**

- For an accident: what came out, what you were doing, whether you felt it coming and how long you
  had, whether you made it, where you were, **how full the pad or brief was** (dry to leaked
  through), its weight and so how much was lost, changed clothes or bedding, sore skin, and signs
  of an infection, with a nudge to see someone. Also bowel accidents, wet nights, catheters, and
  your own words, tags and fields.
- **Supplies**: the pads, briefs, diapers, bed pads, reusable underwear, catheters, wipes and
  period products you have, counted down as you log changes, with how long they'll last and a
  reminder before you run out.
- **Is it getting better or worse?** The last four weeks against the four before, week by week,
  with what went along with the change.
- **Bladder training** with reminders that space out over time (with discreet words on a locked
  screen), and weekly check-ins on how much it bothers you.

**Cycle, your way:** products used, clots, colour, where it hurts, mood, energy, libido, sex,
cervix, LH and pregnancy tests and discharge; your own symptoms, moods and fields; your own cycle
and period lengths; reminders before a period and when it's late; and **neutral wording** that
leaves out fertility talk.

**Autism & ADHD**, in Mind, in your words: an "about me" to show people who support you (senses,
how you like to communicate, what helps, accommodations), times things got too much and what
helped, an energy budget and social battery, masking and executive-function check-ins, a focus
timer and body doubling, routines, communication cards, a calm mode, and gentle patterns,
including a note if burnout looks to be building.

**Transition**, at your pace, whatever it means to you: pronouns, milestones with checklists for
documents, changes you notice, voice practice with a pitch meter on your own device (nothing is
recorded), hair removal, and moments of euphoria and dysphoria.

**Progress photos**, for transition, weight or anything else: albums and poses, a ghost of the
last photo to line up the next (in the phone app), and comparisons with the weight and time on
hormones then. Encrypted, stripped of location and camera details, never shown to AI, and shared
only if you share Photos. **Body measurements** (waist, hips, chest and more) chart with weight.

**To other apps:** the phone app can write weight, body fat, blood pressure, glucose, water, food
and workouts to **Health Connect**, where Samsung Health, MyFitnessPal, MyNetDiary and Google Fit
read them, and workouts and weight export as **FIT** files for Garmin Connect, Strava and others.

**An optional AI assistant.** Off unless your server's admin adds an Anthropic API key, and then
each person decides. Before anyone uses it, it says plainly that what it looks at is sent to
Anthropic, a company outside your server, with links to Anthropic's terms and privacy policy. You
choose which features it may look at (private ones are never chosen for you), it never sees
photos, it shows what it looked at for each answer, and you can stop at any time. Leaving it off
is a perfectly good choice.

**The phone app catches up** with screens for blood pressure and glucose, drinks, symptoms,
habits, hormone therapy, life stages, appointments, sharing, reminders, the digest, reports and the
watch.

Also in this version:

- Mind's energy budget shows on Today, with or without Autism & ADHD turned on.
- The AI connector has tools for supplies, bladder and bowel progress, conditions, Autism & ADHD
  and transition, each only for links allowed to see that feature.
