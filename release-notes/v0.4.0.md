## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.4.0: your voice, your devices, a calmer Today

**Voice training, free, in Transition.** Choose lighter and brighter, deeper and fuller, or
exploring, and record a starting point. Then follow a guided programme of 8 units and 27 lessons:
looking after your voice, warm-ups, pitch, resonance, vocal weight, intonation, and into everyday
speech (words, phrases, reading, talking freely, even a phone call).

While you practise you see **live feedback**: your pitch against your target range, resonance
from darker to brighter, vocal weight, intonation and loudness. There are also tones to match.
Everything is listened to on your own phone or browser. Recordings are optional and never leave
it. Streaks, weekly progress against your starting point, gentle reminders and rest days come
with it, and vocal health comes first throughout.

**A calmer Today.**
- Your scores sit in a slim strip.
- **Up next** gathers what needs doing now: doses due (with Take), today's appointment, a routine.
- **For you** holds a few insights, each opening the page it's about.
- One row of quick log buttons, with **+ Log** for everything else.
- Features with nothing logged yet wait in one "Also today" card.

**Meal ideas that fit you.** When protein's behind for the time of day, Today suggests meals
**you've actually eaten before**, for the meal that's coming up, with one-tap Add. The Food page
has "Ideas for lunch" (or whichever meal is next) too.

**Snap your food** (with the optional AI assistant). Take a photo while adding food. The assistant
estimates what's there and how much, and you check and change everything before it's logged. The
photo is shrunk on your device and isn't kept by this server. It's only sent when you take one,
and only if you've let the assistant see Food.

**Your Colmi ring brings in more**, with no Colmi app needed: blood oxygen, HRV and stress, and
skin temperature on the R09 and R10. The app turns on the ring's own measurements for them.

**Two devices, one set of numbers.** Wear a ring and a watch, say a Pebble Time 2 through
Gadgetbridge or Health Connect, and the server works out its own numbers from both:
- **Steps:** never counted twice.
- **Heart rate:** merged over time.
- **HRV, blood oxygen and temperature:** weighted towards the ring at night, each compared with
  its own usual.
- **Sleep:** timed by what both devices agree on. If the ring's battery runs out at 3 am, the
  watch carries the rest of the night.

Tap **From 2 devices** on any of these to see each device's value and how they were combined, or
to choose one device for that reading.

**Gentler for eating disorder recovery.** With that condition, there are no calories, macros,
targets or weight trends anywhere. Food is still logged quietly, and meal ideas come without
numbers.

Also in this version:
- The phone app tells you when your server is older than the app (with how to update), and
  explains instead of failing quietly.
- Insights you can tap: "2 doses still to take" opens Medications.
- The AI connector's day includes combined readings and low supplies.
