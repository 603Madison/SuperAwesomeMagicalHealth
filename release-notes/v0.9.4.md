## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.9.4: Undo, and your medication history

**Undo, everywhere you mark a dose.**
- Take on Today, on Care, under + or on Medications now says so with an **Undo** button for a few
  seconds. So does Skip, and Took one for as-needed medications.
- On the phone, a dose you've marked on Medications now has **Undo** next to it, as on the web.
- Undo puts back anything it took off your count of doses left.

**History: when you took or missed a dose.** Medications has a new History section with a
calendar, a month at a time.
- Each day is marked by what happened:
  - a filled dot: all taken;
  - a ring: one missed;
  - a dash: one skipped;
  - half filled: today, with some still to take.
  Each mark has its own shape as well as its own colour.
- The month in a line at the top: "20 of 23 taken · 1 skipped · 2 missed".
- Tap a day to see its doses:
  - anything marked can be undone;
  - one you missed can be marked **Took it** or **Skipped** (for the time it was due);
  - one taken as needed can be removed.
- Go back a month with the arrows. You can move or hide History, like any section.
