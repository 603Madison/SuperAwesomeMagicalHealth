## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.9.0: a new app, built around your day

The whole interface has been rebuilt, on the phone and on the web. Same font, same colours, but it
no longer works like a launcher of separate features: it's organised around what you're doing.
Five places along the bottom, the same on the phone and the web:

**Today: what's happening.**
- Any health alert comes first.
- Your scores come with one plain sentence about the day.
- **Right now** shows only what's live, each with one button:
  - what you're wearing (Change);
  - a dose that's due (Take);
  - food so far (Add);
  - an appointment coming up;
  - your energy;
  - voice practice.
- **Your day** is one timeline of everything you logged and everything your devices recorded,
  newest first: last night's sleep, meals, doses, changes, walks, readings. Tap any row to open it.
- Tap the date to look back at another day.
- The quick-log row, "Also today" and the long pile of cards are gone.

**Body: what your devices measured.** One switch between 24 hours, Week, Month and Year, so
Last 24 hours and Trends are now the same place.
- **24 hours:** last night's stages, heart rate with the night shaded, HRV, blood oxygen,
  breathing, temperature and steps, each against your usual.
- **Week, month and year:** your trends, nights and workouts.
- **Also here:** health alerts and Compare devices.

**+ in the middle: log anything.** It shows what's due right now first: a change, a dose, or
tonight's meal from your Mealie plan. Then everything you track, in your order.

**Care: what you look after.** One card per area, each showing what's going on, not just a name.
Related things live together now:
- **Medications** includes hormone therapy: the next dose with Take, what's left, and your last
  lab.
- **Bladder & bowel** includes supplies: what you're wearing with Change, your week at a glance,
  and how many are left.
- **Transition** includes voice practice with Start, measurements and photos.
- **Also:** appointments with the questions you're ready to ask, food, Autism & ADHD, and the rest.

Whatever's due soonest comes first.

**You: everything to set up.** Your devices (Record tonight for the strap), your week, a report for
your doctor, the assistant, "Where your data goes", and settings in five short groups.

**Also**
- Every page that isn't one of the five has a back arrow.
- Old links still work: Last 24 hours, Trends, Sleep and Activity open the right part of Body.
- Your arrangements carry over.
- Times in Today read the same way everywhere (3:30 PM).
- The server notice for an older server is simpler.
