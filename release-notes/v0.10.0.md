## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.10.0: your medical records, and food from the USDA

**Update the app and the server together.** Older apps don't understand some of what this server
sends.

### Medical records

Connect your hospital, clinic or practice's patient portal and see your own record here. It works
with organizations on **Epic** (MyChart, like Mass General Brigham's Patient Gateway) and practices
on **athenahealth**.

- **Connecting:** go to You › Medical records, search for your hospital or health system, and sign
  in on your portal's own page. Then confirm in the app.
- **Privacy:** your record comes straight to your server. No other company is in between, and
  nothing is ever written back to your portal.
- **What comes in:**
  - lab results, with their usual ranges and anything high or low said in words;
  - vital signs, medications, conditions, allergies, immunizations and procedures.
- **Labs:** lab results also go into Medications › Labs, marked with where they came from, so they
  chart alongside your own.
- **Medications:** any medication in your record can be added to yours with one tap.
- **Staying up to date:** where your portal allows lasting access, your record updates itself once a
  day. Otherwise, sign in again whenever you want it brought up to date.
- **Disconnecting:** remove everything it brought in, or keep it.
- **Never shared:** it's never shared and never offered to the assistant. Where your data goes lists
  it, and you can pause it there.
- **For the server's admin:** Admin › Medical records has one row each for Epic and athenahealth,
  with the redirect address to register and short "how to get these" steps.

### Food from the USDA

Food search used to bring back products in other languages. Now it's built on USDA FoodData Central,
the US government's public food database.

- **Everyday foods come first,** with real portions ("Banana, raw · 1 banana (126 g)"), then US
  packaged foods.
- **Barcodes** are looked up in USDA's packaged foods too.
- **Open Food Facts** is now only a fallback, for when the USDA doesn't know a food. It's off unless
  the admin turns it on, and English only.
- **For the server's admin,** two choices in Admin › Food search:
  - **Keep USDA's food list on this server:** searches and scans never leave it, and no key is
    needed. Everyday foods only is about an 18 MB download (4 MB kept). With packaged foods (needed
    for barcodes) it's about 214 MB (105 MB kept). It checks for new USDA releases monthly, and can
    update itself automatically if you turn that on.
  - **Or ask USDA's online service:** a free key from api.data.gov/signup.
