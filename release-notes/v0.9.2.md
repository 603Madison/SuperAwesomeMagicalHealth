## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.9.2: Energy and focus, built for ADHD

Autism & ADHD's energy budget is gone. In its place is **Energy and focus**, built on what research
actually supports for ADHD.

**Check in, in two taps.** Rate your energy (running on empty … plenty) and your focus (all over the
place … locked in), whenever you like. Add "stressed", "unwell", "overstimulated" or "in a
hyperfocus" if it fits. It's on the Autism & ADHD page, and on + as **Energy & focus**. Quick
ratings in the moment, a few times a day, are how researchers measure how ADHD really varies
(ecological momentary assessment). They're more accurate than remembering at the end of the day.

**Today:** your check-ins on a line from 6 AM to midnight, with your ADHD medication doses marked.

**Your patterns, from your own data,** once there are about 12 check-ins over 5 days:
- **Your best focus hours** and when your energy dips. On those mornings, Today says so: "Your
  focus is usually best 9 AM – noon". Putting the thing that needs it most into those hours is part
  of the best-tested therapies for adult ADHD.
- **Since your medication:** how your focus rises and fades in the hours after your dose. It finds
  your ADHD medication by name, and you can choose another or "none of these".
- **Sleep, medication, moving, eating and stress:** whether each goes with better or worse energy
  or focus for you.

Each pattern shows its averages and how many days or check-ins it comes from. A pattern only shows
when there's enough on both sides and a real difference. They're patterns, not proof of cause.

**"How this works"** explains it plainly and lists the research behind it. It also says what it
can't tell you: exercise's short-term boost has mostly been studied in children and young people,
and patterns about your medication are for your prescriber, not for changing a dose yourself.

**Reminders to check in** are optional and off unless you add times. When one passes without a
check-in, it shows in Right now.

**Also**
- Your social battery is its own slider.
- Mind's own energy budget, for people with Mind but not Autism & ADHD, is unchanged. ADHD and
  autism no longer turn it on.
- Your old energy budget entries are kept on your server, just not shown.
- No more spoons anywhere.
