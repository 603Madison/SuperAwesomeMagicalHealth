## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.1.0: the first version

Your own health server, in the family's Aurora look.

**The server** (Podman or Docker, one container): sign in with Authentik (or any OpenID Connect
provider), with a local break-glass admin that always works. It keeps your wearables' data,
your food log, weight, workouts and check-ins, and works out:

- **Readiness, sleep and activity scores**, Oura-style, each with the contributors behind it,
  against your own baseline (resting heart rate and HRV against your usual, temperature, sleep
  balance, training load, recovery).
- **Insights** in plain words: what stands out today and what would help.
- **Calorie and macro targets** from your resting energy (you choose the female formula, the
  male one or the average of both), your devices' active calories and your weight goal.

**The web app**: Today, Sleep, Activity, Food (Open Food Facts search and barcodes, quick add,
your own foods, copy a meal, water), Body, Trends, Devices (Garmin .FIT import, upload keys),
Settings and Admin. Aurora by default, with Violet, Ocean and Ember, light and dark, and Bold
text.

**The phone app** (Android 9 and later): your days and trends, logging food (with barcode
scanning), water, weight, workouts, nights and check-ins, and the conduit for your wearables:

- **Colmi rings** (R02–R12, Colmi Ring 2 and other QRing rings) directly over Bluetooth.
- **Health Connect** (Garmin Connect, Fitbit, Samsung Health, Gadgetbridge…).
- **Gadgetbridge's database export** (Pebble, including Core Devices' new watches, and much more).

**The AI connector** (MCP): bring your data to the AI of your choice. Ask about your sleep, spot
patterns, log a meal by describing it, and troubleshoot device syncs (gaps, duplicates, raw
readings). Admins can troubleshoot every account and read the server's log.

Server image: `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalhealth:0.1.0`.
