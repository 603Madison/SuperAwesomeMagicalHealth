## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.1.0: the first version

Your own health server, in the family's Aurora look.

**The server** (Podman or Docker, one container): sign in with Authentik (or any OpenID Connect
provider), with a local break-glass admin that always works. It keeps your wearables' data,
your food log, weight, workouts and check-ins, and works out:

- **Readiness, sleep and activity scores**, Oura-style, each with the contributors behind it,
  against your own baseline (resting heart rate and HRV against your usual, temperature, sleep
  balance, training load, recovery).
- **Insights** in plain words: what stands out today and what would help.
- **Calorie and macro targets** from your resting energy (you choose the female formula, the
  male one or the average of both), your devices' active calories and your weight goal.

**Features you turn on** (Settings › Features), encrypted on the server:

- **Mind**: mood, calm tools (breathing, grounding, a quiet timer), PHQ-9 and GAD-7 check-ins,
  a private journal with gratitude and thought records, patterns in what helps, and support
  lines one tap away.
- **Cycle**: periods and symptoms, predictions with their confidence, the fertile window, and
  ovulation from your ring's temperature.
- **Bladder & bowel**: a one-tap diary, pelvic floor exercises, and a printable 3-day diary.
- **Medications**: schedules, reminders, Take and Skip, adherence, injection and patch site
  rotation, stock, and lab results.

**The web app**: Today, Sleep, Activity, Food (Open Food Facts search and barcodes), Body, Mind,
Cycle, Bladder & bowel, Medications, Trends, Devices (Garmin .FIT import, upload keys), Settings
and Admin. Aurora by default, with Violet, Ocean and Ember, light and dark, and Bold text.

**The phone app** (Android 9 and later): your days and trends, logging for every feature, dose
reminders, and the conduit for your wearables: **Colmi rings** (R02–R12, Colmi Ring 2) over
Bluetooth, **Health Connect** (Garmin Connect, Fitbit, Samsung Health, Gadgetbridge…), and
**Gadgetbridge's** database export (Pebble, including Core Devices' new watches, and more).

**The AI connector** (MCP): bring your data to the AI of your choice, with the private features
only where you allow them. Ask about your sleep, spot patterns, log a meal by describing it,
and troubleshoot device syncs.

Server image: `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalhealth:0.1.0`.
