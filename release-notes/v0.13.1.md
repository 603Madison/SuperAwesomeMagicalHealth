## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.13.1: meditation that keeps moving

- **The player counts down each second** ("9:42 left"), so it's clear it's going on through the
  quiet moments between steps. Before, it only counted whole minutes and could look stuck.
- **It never waits on the phone's voice for ever.** If the voice doesn't read a step, the
  meditation carries on with the words shown, and says so. Turn Voice back on to try again.
