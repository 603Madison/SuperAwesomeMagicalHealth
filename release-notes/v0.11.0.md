## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.11.0: food grades, shopping, servings from a photo, and the weather service

**Update the app and the server together.**

**Food**
- **Grades, A to F:** how well a food fits your own goals and conditions, with two or three plain
  reasons ("High in sodium for your blood pressure goal", "Good protein for your goal"). You can turn
  them off in your settings, and they're always off in eating disorder recovery.
- **Shopping:** scan or search a product before you buy it to see its grade, its reasons and its
  nutrients. Keep a shopping list, and compare two products side by side. Open it from Food.
- **How much did I have?** After scanning a packaged food or picking a Mealie recipe, take a photo
  and the assistant suggests how many servings it is. You confirm or change it before anything is
  logged. The photo is never kept.
- **Clear my foods:** remove one of your saved foods, or all of them. Your diary stays as it is.

**Weather from the National Weather Service**
- Air pressure for symptom patterns now comes from the US National Weather Service instead of a
  company.
- Your place is now a ZIP code, looked up on the server itself, so nothing goes out for that.
- The weather service only keeps about a week of readings, so the server saves them each day. Your
  pressure history builds from when you set your place; the older history is gone.

**Where your data goes**
- It shows only what's set up and actually leaves the server.
- It says plainly that admins can't see your health data in the app.

**Admins never see anyone else's data**
- The admin's "every account" connector link is gone. Each link reads only its own account.
- A password an admin sets, for a new account or a reset, is temporary: you choose your own at the
  next sign-in. Settings then shows "An admin set your password on …" for 90 days, so an admin
  signing in as someone wouldn't go unnoticed.
