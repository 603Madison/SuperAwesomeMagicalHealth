## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.12.0: every Body card opens its details

- **Tap any card on Body** (the 24 hours, and Week, Month and Year) to see its details:
  - the latest reading;
  - a larger chart, with your usual;
  - your average, lowest and highest, with when each happened;
  - every hour or day, newest first.
- **Switch spans** on the details page itself: 24 hours, Week, Month or Year.
- **Sleep** lists each night with its stages, and **Workouts** each workout in full.
- **Calorie goal:** the 10% added to resting energy is now called "digesting food", which is
  what it is. Your devices count movement in "active".
