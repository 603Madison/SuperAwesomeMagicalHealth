## SuperAwesomeMagicalHealth 0.12.0: sign in once on your phone

**Update the app and the server together.**

**Sign in once**
- **SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator** is a new member of the family: you sign in to it once on your
  phone, and the family's apps on that phone sign in by themselves. Health now does too. Open the
  app and you're in, as you.
- The first time, the orchestrator asks whether Health may sign in as you. Say yes once.
- The first time, the app also shows which server it's about to sign in to ("Continue as Maddie, at
  health.example.com") and waits for your tap. After that it's automatic.
- The sign-in screen offers **Continue as (your name)** above the usual ways whenever you're signed
  in to the orchestrator.
- **Signing out stays signed out.** The app doesn't sign you straight back in; it waits until you
  sign in again yourself.
- If it can't be done for any reason, you get the usual sign-in, as before.
- **Only signing in goes through it.** Nothing about what you log or look at in Health is shared
  with the orchestrator or your other devices.

**For admins**
- Set the **Orchestrator address** in Admin › Server (or `SAMH_ORCHESTRATOR_URL`), and press **Test**
  to check it answers. Add this server to the orchestrator's list of apps as `health`, with the same
  address people use for it. Left blank, nothing changes.
- People get the same account they have here already. With single sign-on, the same rules apply as
  when they sign in through it: your allowed group, your admin group, and whether accounts are made
  on first sign-in. Without it, they're matched by username. The break-glass admin never signs in
  this way, and accounts that are turned off stay off.
- Refused sign-ins are logged with the reason, and count towards the sign-in limits like wrong
  passwords.
